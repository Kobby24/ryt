Gelcon – 2008

Gelcon ltd is a partnership between a Ghanaian company, Sarcon quarry Limited (RYB Construction Company group) and Gellini International Limited (Gellini group).

Gelcon Ltd operates in paving industry with the highest quality standards since its partner Gellini works in Italy, almost exclusively, with Public Administrations and with companies in charge of managing highways and freeways maintenance and construction.

Gelcon Ltd manufactures and processes any kind of asphalt mix: from the most traditional ones to Open Grade Friction Courses and Splittmastix Asphalts.

What We Do

  • Gelcon Ltd is committed to bring European state of the art technology in terms of asphalt production and roads construction to Ghana: with a Marini 220 tons/hour capacity asphalt plant, Gelcon Ltd represents today the biggest offer in terms of asphalt production in Ghana.
  • The cold feeders "six pack" assures highest quality standard thanks to the possibility of using, at the same time, six different aggregates' sizes.
  • Other very important features are two filler’s silos, to be used with lime and/or cement in order to guarantee maximum performance to the binding agent and to the mix, and a 90 tons hot mix stock silo, to guarantee our customers a continuous supply avoiding expensive waiting time; our customers can rely on three x 50 cubic meters bitumen tanks, to be sure that never run out, so the asphalt supply is always guaranteed!
  • Last but not least, our asphalts’ quality is constantly monitored and assured by our internal laboratory.
